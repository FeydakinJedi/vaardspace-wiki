Bilgar was an orkish warrior from the desert wasteland of Vaardland. He rode a giant bear named Zoe. Bilgar, unsurprisingly to y, came from the Hilgar region of Vaardland. At the time of his joining, he had been searching for a way to restore the magical streams that once fed Hilgar and befriended an orphan bear he named Zoe.

Bilgar and Zoe journeyed with the Heroes beyond their crystal sphere to battle pirates and Nezznara. He returned home to Vaardland with the heroes and set out again for Hilgar, hearing of a group of adventurers who had discovered the key to Hilgars springs.

Bilgar found the adventurers and helped them defeat an undead monstrosity that had stolen the springs power.

With the springs restored, Bilgar rallied his clan and began rebuilding Hilgar. Other clans joined him and within ten years, the land was restored to an oasis.

Bilgar then disappeared from Vaardland. His whereabouts are unknown, but scriers claim he and Zoe are warlords in the upper layer of hell.
