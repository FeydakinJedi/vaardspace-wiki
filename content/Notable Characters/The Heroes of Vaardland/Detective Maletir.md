Maletir is an orcish ranger who was a private investigator on Vaardland. He was known for his heavy longbow, his distinctive poncho and hat, and his direct manner.

After unearthing the hidden Warforged in Harborcrown, Maletir set out on other cases. He has directly or indirectly contributed to uncovering Neoji and Drow operatives, finding stolen goods, and freeing trafficked people.

Maletir has been spotted in many places throught the galaxy. His current whereabouts are unknown.
