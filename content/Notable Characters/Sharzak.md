Sharzak is a renowned girhyanki pirate who loves to brag about his deeds. He is wanted by the Emerald Armada, Neoji Hegemony, Nephil, and Dumathoins Prospectors for ceimes relating to piracy.

He operates a ship called The Calamari Buzzard, which is essentially a large space caring speedboat with overpowered weapons welded to the hull. He and his crew are known for the daring heists of high value objects that tend to be sold at auction on [[The Rock of Bral]].

Sharzak has a deep hatred of illithid and has been known to abandon a heist or tram up with an enemy to kill mind flayers.
