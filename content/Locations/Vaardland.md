title: Vaardland
description: The island in space
---
Vaardland, or Vaard, is a continental landmass floating on an ocean in a crystal sphere. The Emerald Armada did not allow ships to enter the stellar sphere containing Vaardland for thousands of years. The Armada never revealed the entire history of Vaard, but it is rumored to be all that remains of the elves' home planet.

Vaardland now is the Emerald Armada's headquarters. A small office of the Armada's high command is in historical buildings in the city of Harborcrown. The majority of the headquarters personnel and staff work aboard The Viridian Citadel, which orbits Vaard L4.

Vaardland is the home of Yvarie Galanodel, Burgle Schmurgle, and Ernchait, the Heroes of Vaardland.

Notable attractions include Nashua's Luxury Palace and The Casino.
