---
title: Erythnul
description: A sideways world of extremes
---
Erythnul is a red giant star in Vaardspace. Erythnul has one populated world in its orbit that goes by the same name. The star frequently and unpredictably ejects coronal mass due to violently changing magnetic anomalies. The danger posed by these anomalies earned Erythnul its name. 

The world Erythnul rotated on an axis that is tidally locked facing the star. Therefore the north of Erythnul is a blazingly hot wasteland and the south is frozen in eternal night.

Erythnul is ruled by an ancient red dragon named Hope Wilts Before Me, who claimed the world as their lair roughly 1,000 years after the Disconcordance. Hope Wilts uses the world to host combat tournaments and has gained a fabulous horde through gambling.

All settlements on Erythnul lie on its single continent, which stretches from  pole to pole. The lair of Hope Wilts is at its north, pointed towards the star. The south pole is home to some of the most spectacular aurora displays in the galaxy. In between are fabulous resorts and casinos operating in tribute to Hope Wilts. 

Some of the wealthiest--and least savory--individuals in the galaxy have estates there's secured by vast sums of gold, specifically, along with favors and other symbols of wealth. Over it's long dominion of Erythnul, Hope Wilts Before Me  has assed one of the most impressive hordes in recorded history.
