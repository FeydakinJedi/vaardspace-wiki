## The Emerald Armada is at war with the Neogi Hegemony
The Emerald Armada declared open war on the Neogi Hegemony in response to Neogi ground forces that were building up on the border and a secret agent who was placed in the Armada forces.

Since then a large influx of refugees have been spreading through and away from Vaardspace. The Armada is attempting to find semi permanent homes for the refugees but the majority of their fleet is being posted along the Neogi border. 

The Star Cogs and Dumathoins Prospectors have allied themselves with the Armada. The Nephilim have not offered an opinion on the conflict, neither have the Illithid. 

The Children of Oshumare have stated they will not take a direct position in this war. They have sent announcements throughout the galaxy warning all wild spacers to not use hyperlanes that are forbidden, and to not keep the war away from their spheres.

## Warforged refugees of the Emerald-Neogi War are fleeing into Vaardspace and surrounding regions.
The Neogi created the Warforged using arcane secrets from another manifold of the Material Plane that appeared in the Greater Sphere after the Disconcordance. Along with soldiers and slaves who have been enthralled by the Hegemony, freed Warforged are seeking homes in Vaardspace.any have joined the Emerald Armada to fight against the Neogi.

## Dumathoins Prospector mining colonies provide large amounts of mineral resources throughout the galaxy.
The Prospectors are currently in trade with Vaardspace, the Star Cogs, Dragonkind, the Nephilim, and independent systems. Most mineral resources came from a Prospector mine.

The Prospectors have also claimed a large meteor made of the rare mineral Orichalcum on Lithicas, in the Klauth sphere.
